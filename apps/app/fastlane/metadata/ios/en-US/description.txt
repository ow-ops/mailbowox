mailbowox is a mail and contacts app that connects to your provider over IMAP, SMTP and CardDAV.

Mail
- Several accounts, read separately or together in one inbox.
- Conversations, search, flags, archive, move and delete with undo.
- Write, reply and forward with attachments, and send from the aliases you add.
- Remote images stay blocked until you allow them, and the HTML of a message is cleaned before it is shown.

Contacts
- Address books from your provider.
- Create, edit and delete contacts. Changes sync back to the server.

Accounts and sync
- Settings are found automatically for most providers.
- Optional sync keeps your accounts on all your devices. They are encrypted on your device before they reach the sync server, which cannot read them. Link a new device by scanning a code.
- Optional new-mail notifications use a gateway that stores your IMAP password and can access your inbox. The notification only says that mail arrived.

mailbowox has no ads, analytics or tracking. The app is in English and French. Gmail and Outlook accounts are not supported yet.
